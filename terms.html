01About the app

PMC Portal is an internal construction project management app provided by Team Hands On Private Limited for its staff and the consultants and contractors working on its projects.

Only people given an account by Team Hands On Private Limited may use the app. Accounts are created by the company admin. You must be 18 or older.

03Your account

Keep your login private and don't share it. You are responsible for activity on your account. Tell your admin immediately if you think someone else has used your account.
