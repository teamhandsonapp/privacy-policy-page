PMC Portal

Construction project management

PMC Portal brings a construction project's daily work into one app — site attendance, daily progress reports, quality checklists, drawings and documents, site issues and meetings — so the office and the site always see the same, up-to-date picture.

Features

Site workforce attendance

Check-in and check-out for site engineers, and planned vs actual workforce by trade for every day.

Daily Progress Reports (DPR)

Record each day's progress, workforce, delays and photos per task, with draft and submit.

Quality checklists

Pre-construction, construction and post-construction checklists for every task, with status history.

Files

Drawings, transmittals, bills, quotes and site photos, organised in folders for each project.

Critical alerts and site issues

Raise and track site issues, and send urgent alerts to the right people on site.

Meetings and contact book

Plan meetings, agendas and attendance, and keep project contacts in one place.
