Frequently asked questions

How do I get an account?

Accounts are created by your company admin. Contact your PMC office or admin.

I forgot my password.

Tap "Forgot password?" on the login screen, or contact your admin.

I can't log in on a new phone.

Contact your admin to reset your device access.

A DPR didn't save.

Check your internet connection and try again. If it still fails, contact support with a screenshot.

How do I request deletion of my account or data?

Contact your company admin or email us.

When contacting support, include your name, project, phone model and a screenshot of the problem.
